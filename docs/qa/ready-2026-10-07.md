## Script fixes

`scripts/qa/blitz.sh`: the files check promoted a hard-coded release `1` (a second push makes `#2`), it now waits
for a new release and promotes the newest; the host-grant park poll went from 30 s to 90 s (the two workers
were busy with other deploys). No product bug behind either.

## Notes

- `stackr job get` and `job log` need `--admin` for org-less jobs (panel-backup, cleanup); not a regression.
- A `params set` or `params rm` at env level redeploys the env's tiles; a promote queued at the same time waits
  behind them (locks), which is why the first G5 attempt ran unparked.
